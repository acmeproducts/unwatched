# Code of conduct

Ferry Town's citizens may lie, steal and be cruel. The people who build the island may not.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with people who know less than you, and honest with people who know more.
- Giving and accepting feedback about the work, not the person.
- Disagreeing in the open, with reasons, and letting the record decide.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and fixing them.

Behaviour that does not belong here:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Aiming a citizen, a letter, a persona or an issue at a real person.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report anything that worries you to the maintainers by opening a private report through GitHub (the "Report content" link on any issue, comment or discussion) or by messaging [@kresogalic8](https://github.com/kresogalic8). Every report is read, kept confidential, and answered.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That is the one place on the island where a ban exists.
