# The Gazette · edition 2 · Day 2 · storm

## The storm took the roof off the mill
*Reported from the harbor.*

The storm took the roof off the mill. No flour from the island; bread costs double.

### Rosa Vidal and Ana Perić seen talking at the harbor inn
Rosa Vidal and Ana Perić talked at the harbor inn. Rosa Vidal: “Have you heard anything about the price of bread?” Ana Perić: “Only what everyone has. Nobody asked me.” Rosa Vidal: “Come by the market later. I will tell you the rest.”
